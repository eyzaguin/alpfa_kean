Join & Apply

Ready to join ALPFA Kean and accelerate your professional journey? Follow these straightforward steps to apply. We prioritize clarity, fairness, and a smooth enrollment experience for all majors and backgrounds.

Eligibility

  • Current Kean University undergraduate or graduate student in good academic standing
  • Open to all majors committed to professional development, leadership, and community engagement
  • No prior chapter membership required

Required Materials

  • Completed membership application form (digital submission)
  • Current Kean student ID number and university email address
  • Brief personal statement (150–300 words) outlining your goals and what you hope to gain from ALPFA Kean
  • Resume (one page preferred) showcasing academic, extracurricular, and work experience

Application Steps

  1. 1

    Complete the online application

    Provide contact details, major, year, and student ID.

  2. 2

    Upload documents

    Attach your resume and personal statement in PDF or DOCX format.

  3. 3

    Review and submit

    Confirm your information and submit the application. You will receive an email confirmation within 48 hours.

  4. 4

    Eligibility verification

    Membership team verifies student status and materials (typically 3–5 business days).

  5. 5

    Welcome and orientation

    Accepted members receive an invitation to the next New Member Orientation and access to member resources and events.
